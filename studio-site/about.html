About Us

Emrashotit Studios is a photography, art direction, and production studio based in Lagos, Nigeria — creating imagery that positions people, artists, and brands for recognition at the highest level.

Exceptional people deserve exceptional images — with an exceptional presentation.

02 — Meet the Studio

git

Ogunfeitimi Opeoluwa Moses

FOUNDER & CREATIVE DIRECTOR

My name is Moses, a creative photographer and visual storyteller based in Lagos, Nigeria. My work spans portraiture, lifestyle, fashion, and event photography — giving me the opportunity to explore people, culture, identity, and everyday experiences through images. I believe photography should be more than just visually appealing; a great photograph should communicate something, preserve a feeling, and tell a story that can still resonate long after the moment has passed.

My approach combines thoughtful direction with genuine observation. Whether I'm working with an individual, documenting an event, or bringing a creative fashion concept to life, I take time to understand the person or story behind the image, then use light, composition, expression, movement, and atmosphere to translate that story into something visual.

For me, photography is ultimately about people. I enjoy creating an environment where my subjects can feel comfortable enough to express themselves naturally, while still giving them the direction they need to create strong, intentional images.

Through EMRASHOTIT, I'm constantly exploring and refining my visual language, with a focus on authenticity, emotion, and visual excellence. I want my work to do more than document what happened — I want it to preserve the feeling of being there.

I don't just want to capture what a moment looks like. I want to capture what it feels like.
